KaWee Help & Game Mechanics

HOW TO PLAY

  1. There are 4 players competing in the game.
  2. A Philippine history trivia question appears on the screen.
  3. Press the Bell/Buzzer to answer.
  4. The first player to press the buzzer gets the chance to answer.
  5. A correct answer earns +1 point.
  6. A wrong answer earns no point. That player must wait 2 seconds before buzzing again, while other players can try.
  7. A correct answer unlocks the jetpack and lets the player move to the next platform.
  8. Long-press the flight control to fly; release to attempt landing.
  9. If you miss a platform, you return to the last platform you reached.
  10. Falling does not remove points already earned.
  11. The game continues until a player reaches 10 points.
  12. The first player to reach 10 points wins and earns +1 Star ⭐.
  13. Bonus Stars ⭐ can also be earned by completing daily quests.

⭐ RANKING SYSTEM

Stars determine your rank. Collect enough Stars to advance.

  • Elite — 6 Stars to advance
  • Master — 8 Stars to advance
  • Grandmaster — 12 Stars
  • Epic — 16 Stars
  • Legend — 20 Stars
  • Mythic — 25 Stars
  • Mythical Honor — 30 Stars
  • Mythical Glory — 40 Stars
  • Mythical Immortal — 50 Stars

⭐ BONUS STARS & ULTIMATE GOAL

Complete daily quests to earn bonus Stars and progress faster. The ultimate goal is to reach 50 Stars and Mythical Immortal 🔥.

📅 RANK RESET SYSTEM

Each season lasts 3 months. At the end of each season, rank progress and Stars reset, and everyone starts the new season at Elite with 0 Stars. Season schedule: January–March, April–June, July–September, and October–December. Previous season achievements may be recorded in a Season History/Leaderboard.
